Privacy Policy — Elise Photos Sync

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Elise Photos Sync is a personal, single-user application operated by its owner for her own use.

What the application does

With the owner's consent, the application connects to the owner's own Google Photos account using Google's OAuth authorisation (scopes: photoslibrary.appendonly, photoslibrary.readonly.appcreateddata, photoslibrary.edit.appcreateddata). It uploads the owner's own photos and videos into her own Google Photos library, creates albums in that library, and moves items it created between those albums.

Data handling

  • Photos and videos handled by the application belong to the owner and are stored in the owner's own Google Photos account. The application does not keep copies of them on any third-party server.
  • The application does not sell, rent, share, or disclose any data to third parties. There are no other users whose data could be affected.
  • No data is used for advertising, analytics, or profiling.
  • OAuth access tokens are stored securely by the operator and are used only to perform the synchronisation described above.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the application's access at any time from her Google Account's third-party access settings, which immediately ends the application's ability to connect.
