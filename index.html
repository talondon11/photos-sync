Elise Photos Sync

Elise Photos Sync is a personal, single-user application. It uploads the owner's own photos and videos from her local library into her own Google Photos account and organises them into albums.

The application is used only by its owner, on her own Google account. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not sell or share anything.
